💡 What is OpenBrowser?

OpenBrowser helps you manage multiple online identities from one computer. Most websites track your browser activity by collecting information about your hardware and software. This is a digital fingerprint. If websites see the same fingerprint across multiple accounts, they might flag or block your access.

OpenBrowser creates isolated environments for each of your accounts. These environments act like separate physical computers. You can assign unique proxies and fingerprint settings to each profile. This keeps your online activities distinct and secure.

🚀 Key Features

  • • Multi-Environment Isolation: Run separate sessions for work, personal use, or accounts that require strict separation.
  • • Fingerprint Customization: Adjust browser hardware information to blend in with standard web traffic.
  • • Proxy Management: Connect your personal proxy services to manage your routing per profile.
  • • Task Automation: Use built-in tools to perform repetitive web tasks without manual input.
  • • Data Sync: Secure your browser settings locally while ensuring performance remains smooth.

🛡️ Security and Privacy

Your privacy remains our priority. OpenBrowser stores all fingerprint data and profile configurations locally on your machine. We do not transmit your sensitive profile information to our servers. Because the software operates locally, you control exactly what data stays on your hardware. Keep your software updated to ensure you have the latest security patches for the underlying Chromium engine.
